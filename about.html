About

Hi, I'm Lester.

I'm a Filipino who's been living and working in Singapore for a long time. I'm a husband, a dad, a tinkerer, and someone who has somehow spent far too many years sitting in front of a computer. 😂

I'm naturally curious. I tend to fall down rabbit holes.

I'll start with something simple like "How does GraphQL actually work?" and a few hours later I'm thinking about resolvers, distributed systems, caching, databases and why someone decided that this was a good way to design an API.

Lately, that rabbit hole has been AI and LLMs. I've been playing around with AI-assisted coding, MCP and different ways of using AI to work with software. I'm fascinated by it - not because I think AI is going to magically replace engineers, but because I think we're still very early in figuring out what it actually means to work alongside these systems.

Outside of technology, most of my attention goes to my family. Being a dad has a funny way of putting everything else into perspective. There are things that matter a lot at work that suddenly seem pretty insignificant when you're sitting at home with your family.

I enjoy travelling, discovering new places to eat, messing around with gadgets and technology, and occasionally spending way too much time trying to make something work simply because I refuse to accept that I can't figure it out.

I'm also a bit of a retro-gaming nerd. I like old games, handhelds and the whole idea of being able to carry a ridiculous amount of gaming history in something that fits in your pocket.

I like learning things even when I don't necessarily have a reason to.

Sometimes that means reading about a technology I'll never use. Sometimes it's finance, investing, business, or figuring out how some company actually makes money. Sometimes it's just a completely random question that gets stuck in my head.

That's probably the thing that hasn't changed about me after all these years:

I'm curious.

I like understanding how things work, whether it's a payment system, an AI model, a business, a gadget, or just some completely random thing I happened to think about that day.

The software engineer part of me is just one expression of that curiosity.

If you're here because you're interested in the professional side of what I do, you can find that part of my story on my Resume page.

That's where I keep the more serious stuff - the companies I've worked with, the systems I've built, the technologies I've worked with, and the things I've learned along the way.

And if you'd like to work with me, talk about an opportunity, collaborate on something, or just say hello, feel free to reach out through the Contact page.
