About

Beyond the resume titles - a bit about how I work and what I care about.

Hi - I'm Lester. I've spent nearly two decades in Singapore building the unglamorous-but-critical parts of digital products: payment rails, wallets, remittance, vouchers, and the backend platforms that keep them reliable.

I like work that sits at the intersection of system design and delivery - clear APIs, sensible architecture, and teams that can ship without drama. Mentoring engineers and partnering with Product and SRE is as much a part of the job for me as writing Go services.

These days I'm also exploring MCP (Model Context Protocol) and large language models, figuring out how AI-assisted workflows can make backend engineering faster without cutting corners on quality.

If you want the full professional trail - roles, tech, and recommendations - that's on the Resume page. To say hello, head to Contact.
